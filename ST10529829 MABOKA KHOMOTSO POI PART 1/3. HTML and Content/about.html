About Urban Crust Bakery

Our Story

Urban Crust Bakery is a fictional small bakery serving the local community with freshly baked bread, cakes, cupcakes, pastries and customised celebration cakes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality baked products at reasonable prices while delivering friendly and reliable customer service.

Our vision is to become a trusted local bakery recognised for quality products, creative cakes and convenient customer service.
